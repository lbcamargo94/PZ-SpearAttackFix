description=[h2]A causa raiz[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Bug 1:[/b] decompilamos o jogo pra encontrar a origem exata: em zombie.CombatManager.pressedAttack(), quando o alvo está a mais de 1.25 tiles de distância E [i]isolado[/i] (sem outro personagem em 1.7 tiles), o jogo força AttackType.OVERHEAD (o golpe lento por cima). Perto de obstáculos como cercas altas, esse cálculo dispara incorretamente mesmo com o zumbi bem próximo, do outro lado da cerca.
description=
description=[b]Bug 2:[/b] o jogo varre TODAS as janelas do mapa carregado a cada frame (mesmo sem o jogador atacar de verdade, só pra destacar alvos na tela) — sem nenhuma checagem de distância. Pra cada janela barricada, pergunta se a lança consegue ultrapassar a barricada, e essa checagem já seta a animação de estocada como efeito colateral, mesmo a dezenas de tiles de distância. Confirmado ao vivo: uma janela barricada a 62 tiles disparava a estocada continuamente.
description=
description=[h2]O que o mod faz[/h2]
description=
description=Usa patches de bytecode Java (via [b]ZombieBuddy[/b]): intercepta o método que define o tipo de ataque e, quando a arma é uma lança, troca o golpe por cima e a estocada pelo ataque padrão — a [b]fatiada[/b]. Como o tipo do ataque muda de verdade (não só a animação), o jogo usa a detecção de acerto da própria fatiada e o dano sai normalmente, inclusive através de cercas e janelas. Também intercepta a checagem de barricada de janela, pulando-a quando a janela está fora de qualquer alcance realista de arma corpo a corpo. Outras armas (1 mão, 2 mãos, pesadas) não são afetadas.

description=[h1]EN - Spear Attack Fix[/h1]
description=
description=[b]v2.4.0: spears now ALWAYS use the slash attack.[/b] The overhead swing and the stab become a slash in every situation — near fences and windows, against distant zombies and in the open —, with the slash's damage and critical hit.
description=
description=Fixes, at the root, two spear combat bugs: (1) near certain obstacles (tall metal fences, for example), the game forces a much slower [i]overhead[/i] attack, and the hit frequently fails to register — the sound plays, but the zombie takes no damage; (2) the stab animation can trigger [i]in mid-air[/i], with no zombie or obstacle visible nearby, caused by a barricaded window anywhere on the loaded map, even dozens of tiles away. Bug 1 reported by the community since Build 42.18, with no official fix yet.
description=
description=[h2]The root cause[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Bug 1:[/b] we decompiled the game to find the exact origin: in zombie.CombatManager.pressedAttack(), when the target is more than 1.25 tiles away AND [i]isolated[/i] (no other character within 1.7 tiles), the game forces AttackType.OVERHEAD (the slow overhead swing). Near obstacles like tall fences, this calculation misfires even when the zombie is right there, on the other side of the fence.
description=
description=[b]Bug 2:[/b] the game scans EVERY window on the currently loaded map every frame (even when the player isn't actually attacking, just for target highlighting) — with no distance check at all. For every barricaded window, it asks whether the spear can bypass the barricade, and that check alone sets the stab animation as a side effect, even dozens of tiles away. Confirmed live: a barricaded window 62 tiles away kept triggering the stab continuously.
description=
description=[h2]What this mod does[/h2]
description=
description=Uses Java bytecode patches (via [b]ZombieBuddy[/b]): intercepts the method that sets the attack type and, when the weapon is a spear, replaces the overhead swing and the stab with the default attack — the [b]slash[/b]. Since the attack type really changes (not just the animation), the game uses the slash's own hit detection and damage lands normally, including through fences and windows. It also intercepts the window-barricade check, skipping it when the window is outside any realistic melee range. Other weapons (1-handed, 2-handed, heavy) are not affected.
description=
description=[b]Required dependency:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] must be installed and enabled.
description=
description=[h2]What is not affected (on purpose)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Finishing attack on a downed zombie — separate mechanism
description=[*] Charging attack while sprinting — unrelated to the bug
description=[*] Weapons that are not spears — keep the game's normal attacks
description=[/list]
